sidebarTitle: "Quick start"

---

Setup takes under two minutes. When you're done, you'll have a live booking page clients can use to schedule time with you. No code required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.tikk.chat/register](https://app.tikk.chat/register). Every new account gets a **7-day Pro trial**, no credit card required. During the trial you have unlimited services, unlimited calendars, custom branding and everything else in Pro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim your username">
    Under **Settings → Profile**, pick a username. Your booking page lives at `tikk.chat/yourname` and that username appears in every link you share, so pick something recognisable. Your booking page isn't public until you set one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your weekly hours">
    Click **Availability** in the sidebar and set the hours you're open each day. Nobody can book you outside them. See [Set your availability](/guides/availability-setup).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first service">
    Go to **Services → New service** and fill in:

    - **Title**. What you're offering, like "Discovery call" or "60-min coaching session".
    - **Durations**. The lengths people can book.
    - **Pricing**. Free or an amount to collect at booking.
    - **Description** (optional). What clients should expect.

    You can add as many services as your plan allows and edit them any time.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a calendar">
    Click **Calendar** in the sidebar and connect at least one:

    - **Google Calendar**
    - **Microsoft Outlook**
    - **Apple iCloud**

    <Note>
      Tikk checks **every** connected calendar before showing a slot. If any calendar has a conflict, the slot won't appear. No double bookings.
    </Note>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how your page looks">
    Click **Website** in the sidebar. Two modes publish to the same address:

    - **Classic**. The booking page on its own: your photo, a short introduction and a calendar. Always on.
    - **Website**. A full landing page in front of it, with a hero, your story, testimonials and an FAQ. Switch it on when you want it.

    Either way you edit the real page rather than a form: click a headline and type. Four themes and every colour control are free. See [Your public page](/concepts/booking-page).

  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your booking link">
    Copy your booking URL (`tikk.chat/yourname`) from the dashboard and share it wherever clients find you:

    - Email signature
    - LinkedIn or social bio
    - Proposals or invoices
    - Your website (see [Embed widget](/embed) to add it inline)

    You can also share a direct link to a single service. Find it on the service page.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When someone opens your page, they see your open slots, pick one and fill in
  their details. Depending on your settings they get an instant confirmation or
  wait for manual approval (Pro). Either way, both parties get a calendar
  invite.
</Note>

<Tip>
  For video meetings, Tikk creates a unique link for every booking. Pick
  **Google Meet**, **Microsoft Teams** or **Jitsi** under **Settings → Profile →
  Conferencing** or leave it on **Auto**. See [Meeting
  locations](/guides/meeting-locations).
</Tip>
