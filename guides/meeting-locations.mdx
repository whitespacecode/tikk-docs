Every service has a location and it decides what the booker sees before the meeting and what lands in the calendar invite.

## Choose a location

Set it per service, under **Services → your service → Advanced → Booking Location**. Three options:

| Location              | What happens                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Video Meeting**     | The booking gets a video link (a new one, or your own room) in both calendar invites.       |
| **In-Person Meeting** | You enter an address. It goes on the booking and into the calendar event.                   |
| **No Location**       | Nothing is attached. Use it when you'll arrange the details yourself or when you call them. |

For in-person meetings you can also decide whether the address is **shown publicly** on your booking page or only revealed to people who've actually booked.

<Tip>
  Beauty professionals, therapists and anyone with a studio want **In-Person**
  with the address hidden until booking. It keeps a working address off the
  public web while still putting it in the confirmed invite.
</Tip>

If a service has no location of its own, Tikk falls back to the default location on your booking page.

## Video providers

Video tools come in two kinds:

| Kind                     | Tools                                         | What the booker gets                                                  |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New room per booking** | Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Jitsi           | A fresh link for every booking, created by Tikk.                      |
| **Your own room**        | Whereby, Proton Meet, Zoom, Jitsi on your own server | The room link you paste in. Every booking that uses it joins the same room. |

<VideoRoomPreview />

You set a default for all your services, then change it for the services that should use something else.

### Your default video tool

Pick it under **Settings → Profile → Conferencing**:

| Setting              | What you get                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Auto**             | Follows the calendar bookings are added to: Google Meet for a Google calendar, Teams for an Outlook calendar or Jitsi otherwise. The default. |
| **Google Meet**      | A Meet link. Requires bookings to be added to a Google calendar.                                                                              |
| **Microsoft Teams**  | A Teams link. Requires bookings to be added to an Outlook calendar.                                                                           |
| **Jitsi**            | A Jitsi room. Needs no connected account at all.                                                                                              |
| **Own meeting link** | Pick Whereby, Proton Meet, Zoom or Jitsi and paste your room. Every video booking gets that one room.                                        |

Google Meet and Teams links are created together with the calendar event, which is why they depend on the calendar you picked under **Availability → Calendars → Calendar to add events to**. See [Calendar sync](/guides/calendar-sync#choose-the-calendar-bookings-are-added-to).

### A different tool or room per service

Each service can use its own tool. There are two places to set it.

**From the service.** Go to **Services → your service → Advanced → Booking Location**, choose **Video Meeting** and pick a **Video tool**. **Default** follows your setting above. For Whereby, Proton Meet, Zoom or your own Jitsi server, paste the room link too.

**From Apps.** To give several services the same tool at once, open **Apps**, pick the tool and click **Install** (or **Edit services** if it's already in use):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter a default room">
    Paste your room link. Every service you tick uses it, unless you give one
    its own room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the services">
    Tick the services that should meet in this tool. Each one shows what it
    uses now and what it will switch to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a service its own room (optional)">
    Click **Use a different room** under a ticked service and paste its link.
    **Use default** puts it back on the default room.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The bar at the bottom counts the services and rooms before you save, for
    example "2 services will use Whereby · 2 rooms".
  </Step>
</Steps>

A service you untick goes back to your default video tool. A service that was in person or had no location becomes a video meeting when you tick it.

<Tip>
  Give a service its own room when you want to keep clients apart, for
  example a free intro call in one room and paid coaching in another. Turn on a
  waiting room or "knock to enter" in your video tool, so a booker who arrives
  early doesn't walk in on the previous meeting.
</Tip>

Room links must start with `https://` and belong to the tool you picked: a Whereby room on `whereby.com`, a Zoom room on `zoom.us` or `zoom.com`, a Proton Meet room on `proton.me`. A Jitsi room can be on any server.

With a new-room tool, every booking gets its own link. With your own room, every booking for that service shares the room you set. Either way, the link in the booker's invite is the same one as on the event in your calendar.

<Note>
  If your chosen provider can't produce a link (say you picked Google Meet but
  bookings go to an Outlook calendar, or your Google connection has expired),
  Tikk falls back to Jitsi rather than sending out an invite with no way to
  join. Remove or pause your last Google or Outlook account and a Google Meet
  or Teams choice switches back to **Auto** on its own.
</Note>

## Where the link comes from

The link is generated when a booking is **confirmed**, not when it's requested. So a pending request has no meeting link yet and one appears the moment you accept.

<Warning>
  On a paid booking that hasn't been paid, the link in the booker's email is the
  **payment link**, not the meeting link. The meeting link follows once the
  payment clears.
</Warning>

## Changing a location later

Editing a service's location changes it for future bookings. Bookings that are already confirmed keep the location they were made with, so nobody turns up somewhere you've since stopped using.

Rescheduling a booking re-resolves its location from the service as it stands now. See [Manage bookings](/guides/manage-bookings).
