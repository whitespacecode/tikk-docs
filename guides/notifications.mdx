Tikk emails both sides of a booking. Your account settings decide which emails reach you. Each service decides what its bookers get.

## Your preferences

Go to **Settings → Notifications** to choose what you receive.

| Setting                  | What it controls                                                    |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New meeting requests** | An email when someone submits a request on your booking page.       |
| **Booking updates**      | An email when a booking is confirmed.                               |
| **Meeting reminders**    | An email 24 hours before each scheduled meeting.                    |
| **Setup reminders**      | Occasional nudges to finish your profile and set your availability. |
| **Newsletter**           | Product updates, tips and news from Tikk.                           |

<Warning>
  If you use manual approval, leave **New meeting requests** on. It's the only
  thing telling you a request is waiting and unanswered requests get declined
  automatically once their slot has passed.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Payment receipts and other transactional emails are always sent, whatever
  these settings say. They're records, not notifications.
</Note>

## What the person booking receives

These are the emails that make the booking work: a confirmation with the meeting link, a payment link for a paid service you accepted, a note when you decline, reschedule or cancel and a reminder 24 hours before. After a payment, the booker also gets a receipt.

Your account settings don't touch them. Each service has its own **Notifications** section instead, where you can switch off the booker reminder and, on Pro, reword the confirmation, reminder, new-time, cancellation and decline emails. See [Booker notifications and emails](/guides/booker-emails).

On the Free plan, the booker's emails carry the Tikk logo and a "Made with tikk" band at the bottom. On Pro, switch on **Hide Tikk branding** and they show your name instead, in the colour of your booking page, without the band.

## Reminders

The 24-hour reminder goes to **both** of you. Yours can be switched off under **Meeting reminders**. The booker's can be switched off per service, under the service's **Notifications**.

<Tip>
  The reminder is the single biggest lever on no-shows. If you're tempted to
  turn yours off because your calendar already reminds you, leave it on anyway.
  It's also the moment you'd notice a booking you meant to move.
</Tip>

## Language

Tikk speaks English, Dutch and French. Set yours under **Settings → Appearance → Language**; it applies to the app, the emails you receive, the emails your bookers receive and the description of the events Tikk adds to your calendar.
