Every service has its own **Notifications** section. Open the service under **Services** and pick **Notifications** in its sidebar. It shows the emails your bookers get, in the order a booking lives through them. You can also choose what bookers may change themselves.

| Stage                             | Email            | Sent                                       |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **When someone books**            | **Confirmation** | Once the booking is confirmed              |
| **When you review a request**     | **Decline**      | When you decline a request                 |
| **Reminder before the booking**   | **Reminder**     | 24 hours before the meeting                |
| **If the booking is rescheduled** | **New time**     | When you move the booking to another time  |
| **If the booking is cancelled**   | **Cancellation** | When you cancel the booking                |

Click a stage to open it, then click its email to edit it. A stage with a dashed outline doesn't send anything at the moment, or is closed to bookers.

## Let bookers reschedule or cancel

By default, bookers can move or cancel a booking themselves, up to 24 hours before it starts. The confirmation, the reminder and the new-time email end with a **Reschedule · Cancel** line. Bookers pick a new time from this service's availability, or cancel with an optional message.

Use the switches on the **If the booking is rescheduled** and **If the booking is cancelled** stages to turn either one off. When at least one is on, the open stage shows how late a booker can still make the change: until the start, or 1, 12, 24 or 48 hours before it.

<Warning>
  The switches apply straight away, including to the links in emails your
  bookers already have. Tikk asks you to confirm before turning one off.
</Warning>

A booker can't always make a change, even with it switched on:

- **Paid bookings** can be rescheduled, but not cancelled. There's no refund flow yet, so the booker asks you instead.
- **Group services** can't be changed by bookers at all, because everyone shares one session.
- **Past the cutoff**, the booking page tells the booker it's too late to change online and that the meeting is still on.

When a booker changes a booking, your calendar is updated and the old slot opens up again. The booker gets a short confirmation of what they did. Your own reworded new-time or cancellation email isn't used, since it's written as your change. You get an email about it too, unless you've switched off **Booking updates** under **Settings → Notifications**.

<Note>
  Turning a switch off only stops bookers. You can still reschedule and cancel
  from your **Inbox**, and the booker still gets the new-time or cancellation
  email. See [Manage bookings](/guides/manage-bookings).
</Note>

## Switch off the booker reminder

The switch on **Reminder before the booking** stops the 24-hour reminder to bookers of this service. It's available on every plan. Your own reminder is a separate setting under **Settings → Notifications**. See [Notifications](/guides/notifications).

## Reword the emails

<Note>
  Rewording emails is a **Pro feature**. On the Free plan you can open each
  email and see the wording your bookers get, but not change it.
</Note>

Each email has a **Subject** and a **Message**. The message is your opening text. Tikk adds the rest below it: the date and time, the meeting link or location, the reschedule and cancel links and your name. Whatever you write, the booking details are always there and can't be broken.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the email">
    Open its stage and click the email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your text">
    Change the subject and the message. Use **Insert** to add a placeholder
    where your cursor is. A blank line starts a new paragraph.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the preview">
    The preview fills in a sample booking as you type. On a wide screen it sits
    beside the editor, with a desktop and a phone width.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The email shows **Customised** in the list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Placeholders are filled in from each booking when the email goes out:

| Placeholder    | Becomes                                      |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `{first_name}` | The booker's first name                      |
| `{name}`       | The booker's full name                       |
| `{service}`    | The service title                            |
| `{date}`       | The meeting date, on the booker's clock      |
| `{time}`       | The meeting time, on the booker's clock      |
| `{duration}`   | The length, such as "30 min"                 |
| `{host}`       | Your name                                    |
| `{location}`   | The address, or "Online" for a video meeting |

Anything else in curly braces is sent exactly as you typed it.

To go back to Tikk's wording, click **Reset to default**. Leaving a field empty does the same for just that field.

<Tip>
  Tikk's default wording follows your language under **Settings → Appearance →
  Language**. Your own text is sent as you wrote it, so write it in the
  language your bookers read.
</Tip>

### Good to know

- **The decline email** only goes out for requests you review yourself, so it matters once [manual approval](/guides/manual-approval) is on.
- **Duplicating a service** copies its wording. So does a [personal invite](/guides/personal-invites) made from a service.
- **If Pro ends**, bookers get Tikk's default wording again. Your text is kept and comes back when you upgrade.
