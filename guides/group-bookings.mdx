A group service lets several people book the same slot independently. Workshops, webinars, group coaching, classes — anywhere you want a room of people rather than a one-to-one call. Each attendee books on their own and Tikk keeps count of the seats.

<Note>
  Group bookings are a **Pro feature**. [Start a 7-day free
  trial](https://app.tikk.chat/register) to enable them.
</Note>

## Set one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a service">
    Go to **Services** and create one or edit an existing one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on group bookings">
    In **Advanced → Group bookings**, switch it on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the seat cap">
    Choose how many bookings each slot accepts, from 2 to 50.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to show remaining spots">
    Turn on **Show remaining open slots on booking page** to display seats left.
    Leave it off to keep the numbers to yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a price (optional)">
    A paid group service charges **per person**, so each attendee pays when they
    book.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What attendees see

Each slot shows as bookable until it's full. With remaining spots switched on, visitors also see how many seats are left, counting down as people book. A full slot disappears from the page. If someone cancels, their seat comes back and the slot returns.

<Tip>
  Showing remaining spots works both ways. "2 spots left" creates urgency; "8
  spots left" on a workshop nobody has booked does the opposite. Turn it on once
  a session has some momentum.
</Tip>

## Managing a group booking

Each attendee is a separate booking in your **Inbox**, so you can see who's coming and handle them individually. Cancelling one attendee frees that seat and leaves the session running for everyone else.

Everyone gets the same meeting link, the same reminder 24 hours ahead and the same updates if you reschedule.

<Warning>
  Rescheduling a group session moves one booking at a time, not the whole room.
  For a session with several attendees booked, tell them first — moving each one
  individually is a lot of surprise emails.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  For paid workshops, take the money at booking. Pre-paid attendees turn up;
  free registrations for a workshop routinely lose half the room.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Pulling seat counts into your own systems? Availability for a group service
  includes the seats left per slot. See [List
  Availability](/api-reference/availability/list-availability).
</Note>
