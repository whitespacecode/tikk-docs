Tikk checks your connected calendars before showing a slot. A dentist appointment, a blocked focus hour, a meeting someone else put in your diary. Any of them removes that slot from your booking page. There's nothing to configure per event and nothing to remember. It's always on.

## Supported calendars

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">
    Connect one or more Google accounts.
  </Card>

<Card title="Microsoft Outlook" icon="calendar">
  Personal Microsoft accounts and Microsoft 365 work or school accounts.
</Card>

  <Card title="Apple iCloud" icon="calendar">
    Connect with an app-specific password from your Apple ID. Your credentials are stored encrypted.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Teamleader Focus and HubSpot also feed their meetings into your busy times,
  alongside your calendars. See [CRM integrations](/guides/integrations).
</Note>

## Connect a calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your calendars">
    Click **Availability** in the sidebar, then the **Calendars** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a connection">
    With nothing connected yet, pick Google, Outlook or Apple. Once you have a
    connection, click **Connect calendar account** to add another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Tikk">
    Complete the sign-in or for Apple paste your Apple ID and app-specific
    password. Tikk asks for **read and write** access: read to know when you're
    busy, write to put confirmed bookings on your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which calendars count">
    One account often holds several calendars. Under **Calendars to check for
    conflicts**, click **Checking … calendars** on an account to pick which of
    them block your availability. Only the ones you switch on are checked. Your
    main calendar is always checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Free plan allows **1 active calendar connection**. Pro is unlimited. On
  Free with a calendar already connected, **Connect calendar account** shows a
  lock and takes you to the upgrade.
</Note>

## Connection states

Each account under **Calendars to check for conflicts** shows how it's doing:

| What you see             | Meaning                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Checking … calendars** | Working normally. Click it to choose which of the account's calendars are checked.                                                                   |
| **Paused**               | Connected but not checked. Free-plan connections beyond the first sit here. Click **Turn back on** to resume it.                                     |
| **Reconnect**            | The provider revoked access, usually after a password change. Click **Reconnect**; until you do, events from this calendar aren't blocking anything. |

<Warning>
  An expired connection is the one failure mode that can cause a double booking,
  because Tikk can no longer see that calendar's events. Tikk flags it, so
  reconnect promptly.
</Warning>

To remove an account, click the **×** next to it.

If you move from Pro back to Free with several connections, nothing is deleted. Your oldest connection stays active and the rest are paused until you upgrade.

## How it works

Once connected, Tikk checks your calendars in real time whenever someone opens your booking page. Any overlap with any event, even by a minute, hides the slot. No manual sync, no polling delay.

<Tip>
  Connect your personal calendar too, even if you only take work bookings. Tikk
  reads the times you're busy, not what you're doing. Personal event details
  stay private, they just stop people booking over your evening.
</Tip>

## Choose the calendar bookings are added to

When a booking is confirmed, Tikk adds an event with the meeting title, the booker and the meeting link to **one** calendar. Out of the box that's the main calendar of your first connected account. You can pick any other calendar you're allowed to add events to, from any connected account.

<Frame>
  <img
    src="/images/availability-calendars.png"
    alt="The Calendars tab under Availability, with the calendars checked for conflicts and the calendar bookings are added to"
  />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your calendars">
    Click **Availability** in the sidebar, then the **Calendars** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the setting">Scroll to **Calendar to add events to**.</Step>
  <Step title="Open the dropdown">
    Click the calendar that's shown. Your calendars are listed per account, each
    with its colour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a calendar">
    Select the calendar new bookings should go to. Every booking confirmed from
    now on is added there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Want your bookings to stand out? Create a separate calendar, for example
  "Tikk", in Google, Outlook or Apple and give it its own colour. Then pick it
  here. Your bookings show up in that colour in your own calendar app and on
  Tikk's **Calendar** page.
</Tip>

A few things to know:

- **Don't see a calendar you just created?** Click **Checking … calendars** on that account and click **Refresh**. It then appears in the dropdown.
- **Read-only calendars aren't listed.** Holiday calendars and calendars someone shared with you to view only can't receive bookings.
- **Prefer to keep bookings out of your calendars?** Choose **Do not add new events to a calendar**. Bookings still block their slots in Tikk and still reach a connected CRM.
- **The choice falls back on its own.** If you remove or pause the account your chosen calendar belongs to, new bookings go back to the main calendar of your first connected account.
- **Your own events in that calendar.** Tikk's bookings always block their slots. For other events you put in that calendar to block slots too, switch it on under **Checking … calendars**.

Reschedule or cancel the booking in Tikk and the calendar event is updated or removed in the calendar it was added to, even if you've chosen a different calendar since. See [Manage bookings](/guides/manage-bookings).

<Note>
  The calendar you choose also decides the video link. Google Meet links need a
  Google calendar here and Teams links an Outlook calendar. See [Meeting
  locations](/guides/meeting-locations#video-providers).
</Note>
