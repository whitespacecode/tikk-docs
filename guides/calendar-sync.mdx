Tikk checks every connected calendar before showing a slot. A dentist appointment, a blocked focus hour, a meeting someone else put in your diary. Any of them removes that slot from your booking page. There's nothing to configure per event and nothing to remember. It's always on.

## Supported calendars

  <Card title="Google Calendar" icon="calendar">

  </Card>

<Card title="Microsoft Outlook" icon="calendar">

</Card>

  <Card title="Apple iCloud" icon="calendar">
    Connect with an app-specific password from your Apple ID. Your credentials are stored encrypted.

<Note>
  Teamleader Focus and HubSpot also feed their meetings into your busy times,
  alongside your calendars. See [CRM integrations](/guides/integrations).

## Connect a calendar

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Calendar">
    Click **Calendar** in the sidebar to reach your calendar connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a connection">
    Under **Add New Connection**, pick Google, Outlook or Apple.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Tikk">
    Complete the sign-in or for Apple paste your Apple ID and app-specific
    password. Tikk asks for **read and write** access: read to know when you're
    busy, write to put confirmed bookings on your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which calendars count">
    One account often holds several calendars. Click **Manage calendars** on a
    connection to pick which of them block your availability. Only the ones you
    activate are checked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The Free plan allows **1 active calendar connection**. Pro is unlimited.
</Note>

## Connection states

| State                     | Meaning                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Synced**                | Working normally and being checked.                                                                                                                  |
| **Paused**                | Connected but not checked. Free-plan connections beyond the first sit here.                                                                          |
| **Authorization expired** | The provider revoked access, usually after a password change. Click **Reconnect**; until you do, events from this calendar aren't blocking anything. |

<Warning>
  An expired connection is the one failure mode that can cause a double booking,
  because Tikk can no longer see that calendar's events. The dashboard flags it, so reconnect promptly.
</Warning>

If you move from Pro back to Free with several connections, nothing is deleted. Your oldest connection stays active and the rest are paused until you upgrade.

## How it works

Once connected, Tikk checks your calendars in real time whenever someone opens your booking page. Any overlap with any event, even by a minute, hides the slot. No manual sync, no polling delay.

<Tip>
  Connect your personal calendar too, even if you only take work bookings. Tikk
  reads the times you're busy, not what you're doing. Personal event details
  stay private, they just stop people booking over your evening.
</Tip>

## Bookings on your calendar

When a booking is confirmed, Tikk writes an event with the meeting title, the booker's name and the meeting link to **every** connected calendar, not just one. You don't have to copy anything across and whichever calendar you happen to be looking at shows the booking.

Reschedule or cancel the booking in Tikk and that calendar event is updated or removed to match. See [Manage bookings](/guides/manage-bookings).
