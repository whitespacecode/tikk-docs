Charge for any service and take the money at booking. The slot is only confirmed and written to both calendars once payment goes through, so you never have an unpaid meeting sitting on your calendar.

## Accepted payment methods

Clients pay during the booking flow with:

- **iDEAL**. Direct bank transfer, the standard in the Netherlands.
- **Bancontact**. Widely used in Belgium.
- **Credit or debit card**. Visa, Mastercard and other major networks.

Payment happens inline. Clients don't need a Tikk account.

## Set a price

Pricing is set per service, under **Services → your service → Availability**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch pricing to Paid">
    Choose **Paid** and enter an amount. It must be a whole number between €1
    and €5,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide how the rate applies">
    By default the amount is an **hourly rate**: a 30-minute booking costs half
    of it, a 90-minute booking one and a half times. Turn on **Fixed pricing**
    to charge the same amount per appointment, whatever its length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The booking form for this service now includes a payment step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You set one amount, not one per duration. Tikk works out what each enabled duration costs and shows the price next to it, both in the editor and on your booking page. With a €90 hourly rate, a 20-minute booking is listed at €30 and a 90-minute one at €135.

## Transaction fees

| Plan | Transaction fee                             |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------- |
| Free | Platform fee per booking.                   |
| Pro  | **0%**. You keep 100% of the meeting price. |

<Note>
  Pro removes the platform fee on paid bookings. If you take paid bookings at
  any real volume, it usually pays for itself.
</Note>

## Getting paid out

Payouts are **not** automatic. You request them when it suits you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your payout details once">
    Under **Billing**, add your IBAN and the account holder's name. Do this
    before you publish a paid service; without it there's nowhere to send the
    money.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let payments clear the hold period">
    A payment becomes available for payout **7 days after it was paid**. Until
    then it shows in **Earnings** as pending, with the days remaining.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the payout">
    Once your available balance reaches the **€10 minimum**, click **Request
    payout** in **Earnings**. The full available balance is transferred to your
    bank account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A few rules worth knowing:

- You can have **one pending payout request at a time**. Submit the next one after the current is processed.
- Each payout produces a **downloadable invoice**, so your bookkeeping has a document per transfer.
- **Earnings** breaks every payment down into gross, platform fee and net and shows which payments have been paid out.

## When payment fails

If checkout is started but never completed, the booking sits as **Awaiting payment** and the slot is released. A declined payment shows as **Payment failed**. Either way you don't end up with a confirmed meeting that was never paid for.

The calendar event and the meeting link are only issued after the payment is captured. Never before.

## Free meetings

Leave a service on **Free** pricing and the payment step is skipped entirely.

<Tip>
  Even a small price cuts no-shows. Someone who has paid €20 turns up in a way
  someone who clicked a free link doesn't.
</Tip>
