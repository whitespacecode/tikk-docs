## How Tikk works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your booking page">
    Sign up, claim `tikk.chat/yourname` and set your availability, durations and
    prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendars">
    Sync Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple iCloud. Tikk reads all of them so
    visitors only see slots when you're actually free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Someone books you">
    Visitors pick a slot and fill in their details. Auto-confirm or review each
    request before it hits your calendar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Meet and get paid">
    A Google Meet, Teams or Jitsi link is created automatically. Clients pay
    when they book and you transfer your balance to your bank whenever you like,
    with 0% transaction fees on Pro.
  </Step>
</Steps>
