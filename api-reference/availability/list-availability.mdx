---
title: "List your free time slots"
openapi: "/api-reference/openapi.yaml GET /availability"
---

<Tip>
  Pass `service` whenever you know which service is being booked. General
  availability can show slots that the chosen service doesn't offer.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The range is capped at 31 days. Read `meta.to` for the last day answered
  rather than echoing your own `to`.
</Note>
