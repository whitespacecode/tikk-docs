---
title: "List your services"
openapi: "/api-reference/openapi.yaml GET /services"
---

Every service has a `type`:

| `type`       | What it is                                                                                                       |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `default`    | A permanent booking page, such as "Intro call".                                                                  |
| `single_use` | A private booking link for one person, spent once it's booked. Bookings through it are accepted and never paid. |

<Note>
  Don't confuse `type: "default"` with `is_default`. `is_default` marks the one
  service your booking page highlights.
</Note>
