Tikk is a free scheduling platform. You get a booking page at `tikk.chat/username` where clients browse your services, pick a time and pay in one flow. No back and forth.

These docs cover setting up your first service, embedding the widget on your site and using the REST API.

## Who uses Tikk

Anyone who sells their time. A few examples:

**Coaches** sell discovery calls and recurring sessions, taking payment upfront.

**Consultants** run strategy sessions, retainer kick-offs and audits under one link, each with its own duration and price.

**Freelancers** share their `tikk.chat` link in proposals and email signatures so clients can book an intro call when they're ready.

**Recruiters** coordinate candidate interviews across calendars. Tikk's availability checks remove conflicts.

**Tutors** offer free trial lessons and paid packages. Students pick a service and pay at booking.

**Beauty professionals** manage cuts, treatments and consultations, with location details in place of meeting links.

## Security and privacy

Tikk is hosted in the European Union and is GDPR compliant. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. We don't sell or share your data or your clients' data. You own your data.
