## Design is free

Building a good-looking page isn't the upsell. The [website builder](/guides/website-mode) is on the Free plan and so is everything in it: every section, all four themes and every colour control. There are no locked blocks in the editor. A page you're proud to share is the point and a palette that can't be matched to your brand is the first reason to stop building one.

What Pro buys around it is your own logo in place of ours, the badge gone, the booking views that suit a fuller calendar and the room to sell more than one thing.

## Drafts, not disabled controls

The few things Pro does gate are still editable on Free. They're saved and simply not published.

Upload a logo, set your photo shape, pick the Agenda view. None of it appears on your live page and all of it appears the moment you upgrade. You never build the same thing twice and downgrading doesn't destroy it either.

## What 0% fees means

On Free, a small platform fee applies to each paid booking. On Pro, that drops to 0%. You keep every cent.

**Example.** You charge €200 for a consulting call.

- **Free**. You receive €200 minus the platform fee.
- **Pro**. You receive the full €200.

At regular paid volumes, Pro usually pays for itself after one or two bookings a month.

## The Pro trial

Every new account gets 7 days of Pro. Unlimited services, 0% fees, custom branding, group bookings and everything else. No credit card on file.

When the trial ends:

- **Upgrade to Pro**. Add a payment method under **Billing** to continue at €12/month.
- **Stay on Free**. Your account rolls back automatically. Nothing is deleted: your page shows one service, the one you've highlighted, and your oldest calendar connection stays active. Your page is unaffected: every section, your theme and your colours are free and stay exactly as they were. The rest comes back the moment you upgrade.

<Note>

  booking page on Free indefinitely.
</Note>
