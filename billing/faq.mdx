Common questions about pricing, fees and your subscription. If yours isn't here, email [support@tikk.chat](mailto:support@tikk.chat).

<Accordion title="Is Tikk really free?">
  Yes. Free costs €0 and never expires. Not a trial. You can create a booking
  page, offer a service, connect a calendar and take paid bookings on Free
  indefinitely. No credit card required.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the Pro trial?">
  Every new account gets 7 days of Pro from registration. No card needed. During
  the trial you have every Pro feature: unlimited services, 0% fees, custom
  branding, group bookings. When it ends, you pick Pro or Free. Nothing charges
  automatically.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are the transaction fees on Free?">
  Free applies a small platform fee per paid booking. That's how the free tier
  stays free. On Pro the fee is **0%**. You keep 100% of the meeting price. At
  any real volume, Pro pays for itself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I upgrade to Pro?">
  Open **Billing** from the account menu, pick Pro and add a payment method. Pro
  features activate immediately and your billing cycle starts that day. Still on
  the 7-day trial? Upgrading just locks in Pro going forward.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel Pro any time?">
  Yes. Cancel any time. No penalties. Pro features stay active until the end of
  your current billing period. After that your account moves to Free. Your
  booking page, bookings and data are preserved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What currencies does Tikk support?">
  All major currencies. Amounts are stored in minor units internally (e.g.
  `5000` is €50.00). You enter prices in standard units. Tikk handles the
  conversion.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods can my clients use?">
  Clients can pay with:

- **iDEAL** (Netherlands)
- **Bancontact** (Belgium)
- **Credit or debit card** (Visa, Mastercard and others)

Payment processing is handled securely. No separate provider to set up.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When do I get paid out?">
  When you ask. Payouts aren't automatic:

- Add your **IBAN and account holder name** under **Billing** before publishing a paid service.
- Each payment becomes available for payout **7 days after it was paid**. Until then **Earnings** shows it as pending, with the days remaining.
- Once your available balance reaches **€10**, click **Request payout** in **Earnings** to transfer it to your bank account.
- You can have one payout request in flight at a time and each payout comes with a downloadable invoice for your records.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my data safe?">
  Yes:

- **EU-hosted**. Data stays in the European Union.
- **GDPR compliant**.
- **Encrypted in transit and at rest**.
- **No data resale**. We never sell your data or your clients' data.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
  Two options:

- **Email**: [support@tikk.chat](mailto:support@tikk.chat)
- **Contact form**: [https://tikk.chat/contact](https://tikk.chat/contact)

The team usually responds within one business day.

</Accordion>
