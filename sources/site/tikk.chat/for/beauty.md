## What every stylist deals with

### 

Appointment requests arriving through messages

Your time is for the craft, not inbox management. One link in your Instagram bio lets clients book their treatment, select their duration and confirm their slot.

### 

No-shows on unpaid reservations

An empty chair has a real cost. Clients who pay upfront are significantly more likely to attend. Payment at booking is the most reliable way to reduce no-shows.

### 

One slot duration does not fit all treatments

A trim takes 30 minutes. A full colour treatment takes 3 hours. Create separate bookable types with different durations, prices and availability for each service.
