## The admin that gets in the way of teaching

### 

Scheduling individual sessions takes too long

Students see your live availability and book themselves in. No emails, no message threads, no coordination to find a mutual time.

### 

Late payments and unpaid invoices

Payment is collected at the time of booking. Lessons are confirmed only once the student has paid. No uncomfortable conversations about money.

### 

No-shows reducing your productive time

Students who pay upfront are significantly more likely to attend. Paid bookings reduce no-shows without requiring any follow-up from you.
