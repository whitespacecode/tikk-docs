## Common challenges in a coaching practice

### 

Friction between interest and booking

Potential clients lose momentum between expressing interest and confirming a session. Reducing that gap directly improves how many conversations convert to booked appointments.

### 

Discovery calls competing with paid time

Offering a free introductory call supports client acquisition without affecting paid availability. A dedicated discovery service keeps the two clearly separated.

### 

No-shows and last-minute cancellations

Clients who book without financial commitment are more likely to cancel. Collecting payment upfront raises accountability and keeps your calendar reliable.
