Your booking page is your public scheduling hub at `tikk.chat/yourname`. It introduces you and lists every active service you've published, so anyone with the link can book you without emailing back and forth.

## What visitors see

At the top of the page: your name, your title, a short bio, your profile photo and your logo. Edit all of them under **Booking Page** in the sidebar.

Below that, each of your active services appears with its title, duration and price. Clicking one opens its booking flow, where the visitor picks a slot and fills in their details.

<Note>
  On the Free plan only one service is publicly visible. If you have several,
  Tikk shows the one you've highlighted, falling back to your oldest. Upgrade to
  Pro to show them all.
</Note>

## Your page isn't live until you claim a username

A new account has no username and until you pick one there's no public page and no one can book you. Claim yours under **Settings → Profile**. Your booking page goes live the moment you save it.

Choose carefully: the username is in every link you share.

## Booking URLs

Your page lives at your username and every service gets its own URL beneath it:

```text
tikk.chat/{username}
tikk.chat/{username}/{service}
```

So if your username is `joeri` and you have a discovery call:

```text
tikk.chat/joeri
tikk.chat/joeri/discovery-call
```

The last part is the service's booking link, generated from its title. See [Services](/concepts/services) for how to change it.

## Sharing your page

Click **Share** on your booking page for four ways to hand out the link:

| Tab         | What you get                                                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Share**   | The plain link, ready to copy into an email signature, proposal or bio.               |
| **Embed**   | A snippet that puts the booking page on your own website. See [Embed widget](/embed). |
| **Social**  | Pre-written posts for the common social networks.                                     |
| **QR code** | A downloadable QR code, for print, slides or a shop window.                           |

## Branding and layout

Open **Booking Page** and switch to the **Branding** tab. Everything here is a **Pro feature**:

| Setting                | What it does                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Booking view**       | How visitors pick a time: **Month** (a calendar), **Day** (one day at a time) or **Agenda** (a list of the next open times, no date picking). |
| **Custom logo**        | Replaces the Tikk logo on your public page. Max 2MB.                                                                                          |
| **Hide Tikk branding** | Removes Tikk branding from your public page, your emails and the embed widget.                                                                |
| **Accent colour**      | The colour used for buttons, selected slots and highlights.                                                                                   |
| **Page background**    | A colour or a CSS gradient like `linear-gradient(135deg, #667eea, #764ba2)`.                                                                  |
| **Custom headline**    | A message or quote above your booking card, in a colour you choose.                                                                           |
| **Avatar shape**       | Circle, rounded or square.                                                                                                                    |

<Note>
  You can set these on the Free plan and they'll be saved, they just won't show
  on your public page. They activate automatically when you upgrade.
</Note>
