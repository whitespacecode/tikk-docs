A booking is one person's claim on one of your slots. Every booking arrives in your **Inbox**, carries the booker's details and the topic they wrote and moves through a small set of statuses from request to meeting.

## What a booking holds

Beyond the time and the service, each booking records who booked it and their email address, the topic they described, where the meeting happens and its link, any payment and, for group services, the other attendees.

You can also add **notes** to a booking. Notes are yours alone; the booker never sees them.

## Statuses

| Status        | What it means                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Pending**   | A request waiting for you to accept or decline. Nothing is on your calendar yet and the slot stays open to others until you accept.                    |
| **Accepted**  | Confirmed. The calendar event exists and both sides have the details. A paid booking first shows as **Awaiting Payment**: the slot is held, the event follows payment. |
| **Declined**  | You turned it down, or nobody answered before the slot time. The booker is emailed.                                                                    |
| **Cancelled** | You called it off after it was accepted, or its payment failed or expired. The slot reopens.                                                           |

Try it: move a booking along and watch the badge it shows in your **Inbox** and the emails each step sends.

## Payments on a booking

A booking's payment is in one of five states: **none** for free meetings, **pending** while checkout is unfinished, **paid**, **failed** or **refunded**.

<Warning>
  A paid booking that hasn't been paid yet has two different links on it and
  they are not interchangeable. One is the **payment link**, where the booker
  completes checkout. The other is the **meeting link**, the video call itself.
  Sending someone the payment link when they asked how to join is a common
  mix-up.
</Warning>

## What happens without you

Two things change bookings on their own:

- **Reminders.** 24 hours before an accepted meeting, both you and the attendee get an email. You can turn your own off under [notifications](/guides/notifications).
- **Expired requests are declined.** A pending request whose slot time has come and gone is declined automatically, twice a day and the requester is told. It stops stale requests sitting in your Inbox forever and stops anyone accepting a meeting that already happened.

Accepting, declining, rescheduling and cancelling are all yours to do. See [Manage bookings](/guides/manage-bookings).
