A service is a bookable offering on your booking page. A free discovery call, a paid consulting session, a group workshop. Each one has its own booking link, its own durations and price and its own rules. Every booking is made against a specific service.

Create and edit them under **Services**. The editor has four sections:

| Section          | What it covers                       |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Basics**        | Name, URL and description             |
| **Availability**  | Pricing and booking durations         |
| **Limits**        | Notice, booking window and quotas     |
| **Advanced**      | Group bookings and the approval flow  |

## Basics

| Setting          | Notes                                                                                |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Title**        | What you're offering, e.g. "Discovery call". Up to 50 characters.                    |
| **Booking link** | The last part of the URL, generated from the title. Customising it is a Pro feature. |
| **Description**  | What the meeting covers, shown on your booking page. Up to 500 characters.           |

The booking link is the tail of the service's URL:

```text
tikk.chat/joeri/discovery-call
                └──────────── booking link
```

It may contain lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens and must be unique on your account. Rename the service later and the link follows the new title, unless you've customised it. A custom link stays put and it keeps working even if you move back to the Free plan.

## Durations and pricing

A single service can offer several lengths. Under **Booking Durations**, tick the ones visitors may book, anywhere from 5 minutes to 8 hours. If you enable more than one, the visitor picks the length when booking.

Pricing is either **Free** or **Paid**:

- **Free**. Nothing to pay, no payment step in the booking flow.
- **Paid**. You set a whole-number amount between €1 and €5,000.

By default a paid rate is **per hour**, so a 30-minute booking costs half of it and a 90-minute booking one and a half times. Switch on **Fixed pricing** to charge the same amount per appointment regardless of length.

<Note>
  Before publishing a paid service, add your IBAN under **Billing**, otherwise
  there's nowhere to send the money. See [Payments](/guides/payments).
</Note>

## Limits

Find these under the **Limits** tab. They control when a service can be booked and how often, on top of your regular [availability](/concepts/availability).

### Minimum notice

How much warning you need before someone can book you — the gap between right now and the earliest bookable slot. Enter it as minutes, hours or days, anywhere from zero up to 30 days.

On the Free plan every service keeps the default of **15 minutes** and the field is locked. Pro lets you set your own per service: a quick chat might need none at all, while a prep-heavy session might need a full day's warning.

### Booking window

How far into the future a service stays open. Off by default, so there's no ceiling — visitors can book as far out as your availability reaches. This is a Pro feature.

Turn it on and choose one of two shapes:

- **Rolling**. Bookable for the next *N* days from today, counted either as calendar days or business days (business days skip Saturdays and Sundays). Up to 730 days.
- **Date range**. Bookable only between a start and end date you set. If the range hasn't started yet, that start date becomes the earliest bookable moment, later than minimum notice alone would allow.

### Quotas

Two independent caps on how much of a service you're willing to sell in a stretch of time, both Pro features:

- **Limit booking frequency**. The maximum number of bookings in a period, up to 1,000.
- **Limit total booking duration**. The maximum number of minutes booked in a period, up to 10,080 (a week's worth).

Either one can run several periods at once — a day, a week, a month, a year — so you can say "one a day, and no more than five a week." Every active period has to have room, so a day disappears from the calendar the moment any single one of them would be exceeded.

Only **accepted** bookings count against a quota. A pending request hasn't been agreed to yet, so it doesn't hold its slot and can't spend one either. For a group service, a session already on the calendar keeps selling its remaining seats even after the quota is spent — joining a session that's already running doesn't start a new one.

### Bookings per person

Caps how many upcoming bookings — pending or accepted — one email address can hold for this service at a time, from 1 to 100. A Pro feature.

This one works differently from the others: who's booking isn't known until they submit the form, so it can't hide a calendar slot in advance the way the other limits do. Instead, someone who's already at their limit gets turned away when they try to submit another request.

<Note>
  Minimum notice, booking window, quotas and the per-person cap are all **Pro
  features**. On the Free plan, every service keeps the fixed 15-minute
  default notice and no other limits.
</Note>

## Booking types

Under **Advanced** you decide how the service behaves:

| Type                | How it works                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **One-to-one**      | The default. One booker takes the slot and it disappears for everyone else.                                                                                         |
| **Group**           | Several people book the same slot independently, up to a cap you set (2–50). Optionally show how many seats are left. See [Group bookings](/guides/group-bookings). |
| **Personal invite** | A private link for one specific person, not listed on your public page. See [Personal invites](/guides/personal-invites).                                           |

The same section holds **Auto-accept bookings**: confirm instantly, require your approval or follow your account-wide setting. See [Manual approval](/guides/manual-approval). And **Booking Location**: video call, in person or somewhere else. See [Meeting locations](/guides/meeting-locations).

<Note>
  Group bookings, personal invites, manual approval and custom booking links
  are **Pro features**, alongside the notice, booking window and quota
  controls under [Limits](#limits) above.
</Note>

## Managing services

From the **Services** list, each service offers:

- **Highlight**. Marks it as the one to feature. On the Free plan, the highlighted service is the one shown publicly.
- **Duplicate**. Copies all its settings into a new service, for when you offer variations of the same thing.
- **Embed**. Grabs the snippet for your website.
- **Create Personal Invite**. Turns it into a one-off private link.
- **Delete**. Permanently removes it. A personal invite that's already been booked can't be deleted.

You can also deactivate a service instead of deleting it. It disappears from your booking page and keeps its settings and history for when you want it back.

If you drop from Pro to Free with several services, nothing is deleted. Tikk simply shows one of them publicly, preferring the one you've highlighted. The rest reappear when you upgrade. Any Limits you'd already set keep applying exactly as they were — like a custom booking link, downgrading locks the controls rather than resetting them.

<Tip>
  Make your first service a **free discovery call**. A short intro is the
  lowest-friction thing a new visitor can say yes to and it filters out
  enquiries that were never a fit.
</Tip>
